**Confidence on misses (Field 9):** the baseline gives no score, but every missed error was labeled Supported by its strongest rule (`amount_and_keywords_match`: the number *and* the key words matched). These are the card's worst case, a wrong claim that looks fully supported. In the samples the numbers really are in the passage (many are list fragments such as "- Commercial mortgages at fair value: $772 million"); what is wrong is what the sentence asserts with them, or RAGBench's GPT-4 annotator may disagree with the passage. Number matching cannot see either, so these need a human look.

**False alarms:** mostly calculated values the answer derived (percent of total, sums such as "1 out of the 181 leased locations" where the passage only says 81 outside the US) and different wording between the claim and the table row label.
